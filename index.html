Query syntax

Terms are combined with AND. Prefix a term with - to exclude it. Quote phrases: name:"Molten Iron".

A bare term matches names, formulas and descriptions. If it also reads as chemistry — Cu, H2O, SO4 — it matches composition too, so Cu finds Chalcopyrite as well as Copper.
